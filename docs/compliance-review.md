## 2. Federal Alternative Minimum Tax Calculator (Route: `/calculators/amt` & `/fr/calculators/amt`)

### 2.1 Scope & Statutory Calibration
- **Statutory Reference:** Sections 127.5 to 127.55 and Section 120.2 of the Income Tax Act (R.S.C., 1985, c. 1 (5th Supp.)), amended by Bill C-59 (effective for 2024+ taxation years).
- **Parameters Enforced:**
  - Statutory Federal AMT Flat Rate: 20.5% (ITA 127.51).
  - Basic Exemption: Indexed to the 4th federal tax bracket ($173,205 in 2024; $177,882 in 2025) (ITA 127.531).
  - Capital Gains Inclusion: 100% under AMT formula (ITA 127.52(1)(d)).
  - Non-Refundable Credits (including Donations): Restricted to 50% allowance under AMT (ITA 127.531).
  - Carry-Forward Recovery: 7-year deduction against regular Part I tax when regular tax exceeds AMT (ITA 120.2).
- **Provincial Limitation:** Explicitly stated on UI and in footnotes that provincial minimum tax schedules vary across jurisdictions and are out of scope.
