## 01Who is responsible

This website is operated by Ahmed Bello, an independent financial professional affiliated with World Financial Group (WFG). I am responsible for the personal information collected through this website, and I am the individual accountable for how it is handled.

Privacy contact: Ahmed Bello, [privacy@ahmedbello.ca](mailto:privacy@ahmedbello.ca). Write to this address with any question about this notice, to ask for access to or correction of your information, or to withdraw a consent.

## 02What I collect, and why

I collect only what you choose to give me:

- **Contact form:** your first name, email address, province or territory, main topic, and optionally your phone number and a message. I use these to reply to your enquiry.
- **Educational emails (optional):** your email address, if you tick the box on the contact form or use a newsletter signup. I use it only to send educational emails.

Please do not send account numbers, your Social Insurance Number or other government ID, medical details or financial documents through this website. The form is not designed to handle them.

## 03Consent

By submitting the form you consent to my using your information to respond. Consent to receive educational emails is separate, optional and unticked by default. You can withdraw either consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions, by contacting the privacy contact above or, for emails, using the unsubscribe link in any message.

Submitting the form does not create an advisory relationship. If we do begin working together, further information will be collected and used under the notices and consents that apply to that relationship.

## 04Who sees it

Your enquiry is delivered to me by email through Resend, an email delivery service. Resend processes your information on my behalf and may store or process it outside Canada, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law. The website host also handles the technical traffic needed to serve and secure this site.

## 07Safeguards

The site is served over an encrypted connection, form submissions are validated and checked for spam on the server, and message contents are not written to server logs. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, which is another reason to keep sensitive details out of the form.

## 08Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it where the law allows. Contact the privacy contact above. I will respond to a request for access within 30 days of receiving it, or tell you within that time why more time is needed.

If you are not satisfied with my response, you can contact the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (opens in a new tab)](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/) or your provincial privacy regulator.

## 09Commercial emails

I send marketing or educational emails only to people who have given consent, in line with Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). Every such email identifies me and includes a working unsubscribe link.
