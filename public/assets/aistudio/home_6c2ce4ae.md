How the pieces fit

## Most financial decisions don’t happen in isolation.

Paying down a debt, choosing an account and protecting your income all pull on the same dollars and the same years. Seeing the connections is often the first useful step.

Registered accounts connects to

- Saving vs. paying down debt

  Whether to use available room before or after paying down a debt depends on the debt’s interest rate, the account’s tax treatment, and how soon you might need the money.

- Children’s education

  An RESP is one registered way to save for education, and it competes for cash with the RRSP, TFSA and FHSA.

- Retirement savings

  The RRSP and TFSA are both common retirement vehicles, taxed at different moments. Which one comes first can change what you keep.

- Investment risk

  An account is a container. The same account can hold cash or growth investments, so risk is a separate decision from the account.
